About Us

We are the Off-Broadway Players and we are excited to present to you our Fall show: The Cherry Orchard by Anton Chekhov! The Off-Broadway Players, or OBP, is UMass Lowell’s only student-run theater group, encouraging students to take part in the wonderful world of theater, both on stage and working behind the scenes to make every production as successful as it can be. OBP is the oldest club on campus and has been a part of UMass Lowell for more than 100 years. We are an all-inclusive club and encourage anyone who has an interest in the theatrical arts to join us. As of right now, we have a little over 115 active members, and we are always looking for more members! As always, thank you for coming and we hope you enjoy the show!
